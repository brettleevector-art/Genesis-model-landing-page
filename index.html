What is GENESIS

A sound generation system for modern producers.

Instead of browsing presets, users describe sound intent. GENESIS interprets it and generates synthesiser parameter configurations that produce matching audio results — instantly, inside your DAW.

Core concept

The Problem
01 — Friction
Endless preset browsing.

Hours lost searching through hundreds of presets that never quite match the sound in your head.

02 — Interruption
Creative interruption.

Every moment spent browsing is a moment pulled away from the creative state that produces great music.

03 — Flow
Broken flow state.

The gap between imagination and sound creation breaks the flow that defines the best sessions.

Music production should not  interrupt creativity.
The Solution

GENESIS removes friction between imagination and sound creation.

It keeps producers in flow while instantly transforming ideas into usable sonic structures. No browsing. No interruption. Just describe what you hear and receive a preset that matches your intent — inside your DAW, without breaking focus.

How It Works
Step 01 — Input Intent
Describe your sound.
"dark ambient distorted pad"

Type your sound description in natural language — the way you'd describe it to a fellow producer. Any style, any texture, any character.

Step 02 — Processing
Intent becomes parameters.

GENESIS translates your language into precise synthesiser parameter configurations — oscillators, filters, envelopes, LFOs, modulation routing, and effects.

Step 03 — Output
Preset generated inside DAW.

The generated preset appears instantly in your synthesiser's preset browser inside your DAW. Load it, play it, and keep creating — without ever breaking your flow.

Features
01
Intent-based sound generation.

Describe any sound in natural language. GENESIS translates your intent into a complete synthesiser preset configuration.

02
Instant preset creation.

No waiting. No rendering. Presets are generated and available inside your DAW in seconds from the moment you submit your description.

03
Seamless DAW workflow.

A lightweight companion app saves generated presets directly to your synthesiser's preset folder. The preset appears in your browser without you leaving the DAW.

04
Built for modern synthesisers.

Full parameter coverage — oscillators, filters, envelopes, LFOs, modulation routing, and effects — for the synthesisers producers actually use.
